Gayatri is the personification of the Gayatri mantra, one of the most sacred chants in Hinduism. She is known as the Mother of the Vedas and the embodiment of cosmic energy.

Origin of Gayatri Mata

गायत्री माता का उद्गम

गायत्री माता का उद्गम

Gayatri is the goddess of the famous Gayatri mantra from the Rigveda. She is associated with Savitr, the solar deity, and is revered as the Mother of all Vedas. In Hindu philosophy, she represents the divine power that illuminates all knowledge.

The Gayatri mantra, "Om bhur bhuvah svah, tat savitur varenyam...", is considered one of the most powerful mantras in Hinduism, and Gayatri Devi is its presiding deity.
